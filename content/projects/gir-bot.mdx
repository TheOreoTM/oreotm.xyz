## Overview

Gir was a self-funded Discord bot that a friend and I worked on for quite a while. We wanted one bot with the commands and systems that Discord users asked for most. It started as a way for me to test commands and events, and soon grew into a full bot.

## What it did

Gir's command set covered five areas:

- **Moderation**, including bulk message deletion and a `snipe` command to show the last deleted message.
- **Economy**.
- **Levelling**, with rank cards whose bar colour and background each member could customise, plus a profile with stats.
- **Tickets** for support requests.
- **Parties**.

It was listed on top.gg and had its own support server.

## Where it led

Gir is the second of the two bots that came before [Cardinal](/projects/cardinal-bot). Its GitHub repository holds the documentation, patch notes, privacy policy and terms of service; the patch notes cover April and May 2021.
